# Scape — Tone & Creative Direction

The mechanical contract lives in `design.ts`. This is the creative compass: the
register everything (palette, AI personas, the high system, writing, consequences)
should be calibrated to.

## The register, in one line
A coked-out 2D Vice City with the **soul of *Spun*** — neon-gorgeous on the
surface, grimy/manic/paranoid/**funny** underneath. Trashy, kinetic, darkly comic.
Not cool-noir. Not tragic. Absurd low-lifes making terrible decisions.

## Two influences, reconciled
- **Surface (how it looks):** Drive / Miami Vice / outrun-synthwave. Neon dusk,
  pastel stucco, chrome, sunset gradients. The *dream* you're chasing.
- **Soul (how it feels):** *Spun* (2002, Åkerlund). Squalor, jitter, dark comedy,
  pathetic desperation. The *reality* underneath the dream.
- **The bridge already exists:** Hotline Miami = exactly this fusion (beautiful to
  look at, druggy/twitchy/horrible to be inside). We are landing on a proven tone,
  not inventing one.

## The duality is load-bearing
Every system should hold both poles — the high and the comedown:
- **Palette:** neon plazas *and* grimy trap houses. Zones range across a
  dream↔squalor axis.
- **The high system:** coming up = euphoric neon trip; overdoing it = the dark
  side (jitter, sickly desaturation, paranoia, **misperception** — an NPC reads as
  a cop, phantom witnesses, the frame won't hold still). Drugs are risk, not just
  a buff. *This is the most-Spun mechanic we can build.*
  - It is a **cross-system signal**, not a screen filter: high also warps the
    *market read* (volatility looks insane; your trades go impulsive and really
    move thin pools), the *phone* (notifications blow up, everyone's up your ass),
    and the *agents* (they get weirder/more aggressive). Perception always lies;
    erratic high-state behaviour also has real causal effects. Hammering drugs →
    the whole world screams at once. The overwhelmed crash, made systemic.
- **Consequences:** hospital and jail are pathetic and grim-funny, never heroic.

## Register specifics (the writing & feel)
- **AI personas** (`SOUL.md` / `INSTRUCTIONS.md`) are written strung-out, absurd,
  funny-desperate — NOT noir-cool, NOT bleak. The dead-internet posts/DMs are
  unhinged and comic. This is where the agents become *characters*.
- **Editing is the feeling:** hyperkinetic punctuation — snap-zooms,
  micro-stutters, quick cuts on a kill or a deal (Åkerlund's thousand-cut energy).
- **Violence is unglamorous** — twitchy, ugly, occasionally slapstick-grim. Style
  points reward audacity, not elegance.

## Structure: the mundane IS the main event
Spun inverts the usual hierarchy — the strung-out perspective makes trivial errands
feel monumental, so the *side* stuff is the movie. This game does the same, and it
validates the whole architecture bet:
- **The story spine stays thin** — a loose thread, not a heavy linear campaign.
  The weight lives in the emergent AI sandbox (agents, dealing, dead internet,
  the investigation), which is exactly what the systems generate endlessly and
  cheaply. Less authored story, more emergent "main events."
- **The high system is the narrative amplifier, not just a filter.** A delivery
  *while peaking* is monumental; petting a dog *while paranoid* is a whole fraught
  event. The drug perspective is the mechanism that makes small things feel huge.
- **Ambient beats get main-event production** — the hyperkinetic punctuation fires
  on a random street deal, not only on scripted missions.
- The AI agents are not garnish on a story. They ARE the main event; the tone is
  what makes that land instead of feeling like busywork.

## What to resist
- Don't drift to *cool detachment* (pure Drive) — keep it grimy and funny.
- Don't drift to *tragedy* (Requiem) — it's comic desperation, not despair.
- Don't make the neon clean and safe — the prettiness should feel a little wrong,
  like 3 a.m. and you've been up for days.
