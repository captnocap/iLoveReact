One schema, two editors

The property strip and the AI feed both bind to the same global field set. A field changed by a slider or by an accepted card is the same write. No duplicate edit paths.

Tools from the schema

Each editable field auto-generates a set_<field> tool (value + rationale + depends_on), exactly like the house lab — so the AI can only ever propose valid, single-field changes.

Propose ≠ apply

Cards are pending until you accept. Pulsing dots in the strip show which fields have live proposals. Reject a parent → its children orphan. Nothing mutates without your click.

Why no separate preview here

The bottom-right quad already renders the whole world live. This tile's preview is the focused object in isolation — studio framing, not a second world view.
