# Evidence and Public Knowledge

The internet is the world's memory, but public claims are not authoritative
world facts. This mechanic page is deliberately prose-first so design thinking
can exist before every downstream runtime system does.

<mechanic>
  <ref>mechanic.evidence_visibility</ref>

  <fact key="authoritative_source" label="Authority" visibility="author">world facts and runtime story events</fact>
  <fact key="public_projection" label="Public projection" visibility="author">explicit public prose and public facts only</fact>
  <fact key="related_business" label="Example entity" visibility="author">@[biz.cropduster_labs]</fact>

  <public>
Public pages describe what the city can know. They may be incomplete, biased, or wrong; discovery and evidence determine what becomes visible during play.
  </public>
</mechanic>

<notes>
A source fact marked secret is still real enough for mission predicates, but it
cannot enter a shipped public wiki merely because the author discussed it on
the same page. Runtime revelation creates an event-derived public claim; it does
not mutate the source fact's visibility. Designer prose outside public blocks is
author-only by default. This fail-closed boundary prevents the CropDuster
disposal practice from becoming a spoiler through an innocent wiki compile.
</notes>
