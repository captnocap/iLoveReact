4De-magic the shader palette

The real move for your tool: those baked vec3f(...) constants inside each material fn (Rot Siding's paint, Neon Stucco's pink/cyan/violet, Pool Tile's teal/pink/gold) become slots you own. Pick a material, and every color it bakes is exposed and editable — per variant — with the same harmony/library assist filling slots ("a paint that fits this wood"). Seed reshuffles the procedural noise; quality is your D[3] grade. This is the layer that kills the magic-color system.
